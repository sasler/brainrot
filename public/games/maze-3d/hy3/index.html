Neon Labyrinth

Drop into a procedurally generated first-person maze bathed in cyan and magenta light. Sweep up glowing relic shards, then chase the pulsing exit portal before the clock burns out.

Controls

WASD or arrow keys move. Move the mouse to look — click the scene to lock the pointer; if that is blocked, hold and drag to aim. Shift sprints.

Objective

Reach the bright exit portal before time expires. Collect relic shards for score. The minimap only reveals cells you have actually explored.

Movement feel

Smooth wall-sliding keeps you flowing through corners. Sprint to cover ground, but sharp pathfinding still wins the clock.

Atmosphere

Fog, drifting embers, neon wall trim, orbiting relics, and a blazing portal create a moody synthwave maze tuned for fast play.
